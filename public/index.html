If you would rather keep the World ETF

Pairing it with an emerging-markets fund, such as iShares Core MSCI EM IMI (IE00BKM4GZ66, TER 0,18 %, 2.902 stocks), gives the same coverage as All-World and lets you set the emerging-markets share yourself. It costs you a second savings plan and occasional rebalancing.

Two points for an Austrian depot. Selling a position realises its gain and triggers 27,5 % KESt, so pointing new savings at one fund is cheaper than selling the other. Check that any new fund is a Meldefonds registered with OeKB before you buy. Fund data from justETF, 07.10.2026. The overlap is estimated from country and top-holding weights.
